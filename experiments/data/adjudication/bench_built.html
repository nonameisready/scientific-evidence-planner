How to judge (read once)

For each question, search the literature published in the 5 years after its cutoff and decide whether anyone substantively engaged this question.

The bar: at least one paper that investigates this question's own objects, quantities, or claimed relationship. Same topic is not enough — a paper about magnetars in general does not engage a question about the spectral difference between two specific magnetar classes.

Fastest route: the Cited by buttons. The papers that generated this question are listed below it; work published later that engages the question almost always cites at least one of them. That search is pre-filtered to the right year window.

Why this matters: the existing labels come from abstract-level keyword retrieval, which is known to miss engagement with narrow, object-level questions. You are the full-text check. Your verdicts are deliberately blind — the existing label is not shown, so it cannot anchor you.
